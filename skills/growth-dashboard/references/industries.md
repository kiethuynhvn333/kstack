# Industries — suggested metrics and sources of growth

Used by `skills/growth-dashboard/` steps 1, 2 and 4. These are **suggestions** to help a new user
start; they never limit what the user picks. Show the short names first (numbered), then only the
chosen industry's details.

**How to read a row:** *North star* = the one number that best says "we are growing" for that
business. *Typical metrics* = what growth teams there usually track. *Funnel* = the stages, in order,
when the metrics form one. *Sources* = the usual sources of growth, most common first.

## The list (show this to the user)

1. Classifieds marketplace (people list items, others contact them)
2. Transaction marketplace (orders, bookings or rides paid through the platform)
3. E-commerce / online shop (D2C brand or retailer)
4. Subscription app (consumer app, paid plans)
5. B2B SaaS, product-led (free trial or free plan, self-serve)
6. B2B SaaS, sales-led (demo requests, sales team)
7. Lead generation business (real estate, insurance, education, auto, local services)
8. Fintech / banking app
9. Mobile game
10. Media / content / publisher
11. Online learning (courses, edtech)
12. Travel and hospitality (bookings)
13. On-demand delivery / mobility
14. Retail with physical stores (online-to-offline)
15. Community / social app
16. Healthcare / clinics (appointments)
17. Non-profit (donations)
18. Something else — describe it in one line

## Details

| # | North star | Typical metrics | Funnel | Sources of growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Classifieds marketplace | Buyers who contact a seller per day (people with a lead) | Daily active users (DAU), new listings, live listings, leads (contacts), leads per listing, DAU → lead conversion, cost per person with a lead, sellers posting | Visitors → DAU → viewed a listing → contacted a seller | Paid search, paid social, app install ads, SEO, push, email, CRM re-engagement, referral |
| 2 Transaction marketplace | Gross merchandise value (GMV) or completed orders | Active buyers, active sellers, orders, GMV, take rate, average order value, repeat rate, buyer acquisition cost | Visit → search → add to cart / request → order → repeat order | Paid search, paid social, app install ads, promos/vouchers, referral, push, email, partnerships |
| 3 E-commerce / online shop | Revenue (or orders) | Sessions, add-to-cart rate, checkout rate, conversion rate, orders, average order value, return on ad spend (ROAS), cost per acquisition, repeat purchase rate, customer lifetime value | Session → product view → add to cart → checkout → purchase | Paid search, shopping ads, paid social, retargeting, email, SMS, SEO, affiliates, influencers, marketplaces |
| 4 Subscription app | Paying subscribers (or active subscribers) | Installs, sign-ups, D1/D7/D30 retention, DAU/MAU (stickiness), trial starts, trial → paid, churn, average revenue per user | Install → sign-up → active day 7 → trial → paid | App install ads, app store search, paid social, influencers, referral, push, email, organic |
| 5 B2B SaaS, product-led | Weekly active accounts (or activated accounts) | Sign-ups, activation rate, weekly active users, free → paid conversion, monthly recurring revenue (MRR), net revenue retention, churn | Visit → sign-up → activated → active week 4 → paid | SEO, content, paid search, product virality (invites), referral, partners, community |

If the user's business mixes two rows (e.g. a marketplace with paid plans), take the north star
from the one they call "the main business" and add the other row's metrics as options.
